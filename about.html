About Us

Venus K Equipments is a Chennai-based manufacturer of premium stainless steel commercial kitchen equipment. Since 2005, we've been engineering durable, hygienic solutions for hotels, restaurants, food courts, and industrial kitchens across India.

We specialize in custom fabrication — every counter, sink, burner range, and storage unit is built to fit your exact space and workflow. All products use 304-grade food-safe stainless steel for maximum corrosion resistance and easy maintenance.

Why Choose Venus K Equipments

  • Custom Fabrication: Tailored to your kitchen layout and capacity
  • Quality Materials: 304-grade SS, food-safe and long-lasting
  • Complete Solutions: From design to installation and AMC support
  • Pan-India Service: Trusted by clients across Tamil Nadu and beyond
